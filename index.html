About Draw Duo

Draw Duo turns your foldable iPhone into a two-panel drawing studio: a reference image on one screen, your canvas on the other. Import any photo or pick from the built-in library of hand-painted subjects, then trace, grid, or freehand with pencil, ink, charcoal, watercolour and oil brushes. Your drawings stay on your device.

Common questions

Do I need the fold-screen iPhone to use it?

No. On a regular iPhone the studio collapses into a single panel you can toggle between the reference and your canvas. On the fold, you get both at once.

Where are my drawings saved?

Everything stays on your device. Draw Duo has no account and sends nothing to a server. You can export a drawing to your Photos whenever you like.

Can I use my own photos as references?

Yes — import any photo from your library and it becomes a subject to draw from, alongside the built-in painted scenes.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Email us at the address above with as much detail as you can. Screenshots help.

Privacy

Draw Duo collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Drawings and imported photos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded.
